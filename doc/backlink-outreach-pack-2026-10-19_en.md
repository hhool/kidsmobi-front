- **Daily**: 15 minutes on C5/C1; respond within 60 minutes (Email 3 template).

**Expectations** (industry benchmarks, not promises): platform query→citation 5–15%; 50–80% of citations include a link; median first reply to cold outreach ≈ 9 hours, 75% within 38.5 hours — no reply by day 3 is usually a no, so follow up once and move on. Guest posts typically take 2–4 weeks from pitch to publication.

## 4. Email drafts (English, ready to send)

### Email 1 — Guest-post pitch (Tier A)

> **Subject:** Guest post pitch: the 3 checks that decided our toddler's first balance bike
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [Your name], and I run BalanceBikeToddler (balancebiketoddler.com) — an independent balance-bike reference site. We don't sell bikes; we track specs, weights, and safety records so parents can compare before buying.
>
> I'd like to write for [Site]: **"The 3 checks that decided our toddler's first balance bike"** — a first-hand account of how we narrowed a wall of options to one bike, and the three measurements that actually made the difference (inseam vs. seat height, frame weight, and what the age labels really mean). Roughly 1,000 words, original, never published anywhere else.
>
> If it's a fit, I'd include one contextual link to our balance bike size chart (https://balancebiketoddler.com/balance-bike-size-chart/) where it genuinely helps the reader — happy to drop it if you'd rather keep the piece link-free.
>
> Thanks for your time,
> [Name] — BalanceBikeToddler

Why it works: leads with a specific problem and first-hand experience; offering to remove the link disarms editors and raises acceptance; no bragging about site size.

## 6. White-hat red lines (project-specific constraints included)

1. **No buying links.** Sole exception: clearly labeled sponsored/nofollow placements on legitimate sites, counted as brand exposure only, never as a link KPI.
2. **No PBNs, no link farms, no comment spam.**
3. **Guest posts must be original** (targets generally require exclusivity); never syndicate one article across sites.
4. **No invented credentials**: the author bio states the true role (site owner / independent researcher) — never pose as a pediatric expert.
5. **No claims of testing we did not perform**: all site data comes from published specs and retailer-published information; outreach and guest posts keep the same framing. This is the differentiator from most competitors — and the credibility argument.
6. **Only pitch pages that truly answer the recipient's readers' question** — one wrong pitch permanently closes that site.
7. Reddit/communities: participate as a parent; link only when genuinely asked.
